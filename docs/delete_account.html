How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to meqraat.taiser@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your full name and the email address registered in the app.
  4. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers:

  • Full name, email address, age, and gender
  • Account credentials and login information
  • Session history and teacher notes
  • In-app messages and assignments
  • Learning preferences (level, schedule, preferred days)
  • Push notification token (FCM)
  • Device identifier

Data Retention

All personal data is deleted within 30 days of the confirmed deletion request. Audio recordings of sessions are stored locally on your device only and are not held on our servers — you may delete them directly from your device.
